=== DESCRIPTION (max 4000) ===
If you can sound out Arabic script, you can start reading classical texts.

Excerpts from 22 classical works by six scholars: Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn Taymiyyah,
Ibn al-Jawzī, Imam Nawawī and more. Read the Arabic itself, with English line by line.
The audio uses AI voices and the English is machine-translated.

READ WITHOUT DECODING
• Every line of Arabic sits under its English, so you are never stuck on a sentence
• Excerpts on creed, fiqh, sīrah, ḥadīth and tafsīr — sorted by topic
• Sorted by difficulty. Most texts are advanced; 48 are easier.
• The app keeps your place for next time

TAP ANY WORD YOU DON'T KNOW
• One tap gives you the meaning, a grammar note and audio
• For most words, see the root and other words built on it
• Colour shows how multi-part words are built
• Save it to practice without losing your place on the page

HEAR EVERY LINE READ ALOUD
• Queue any text and listen. Every text plays English first, then Arabic, or Arabic only
• Built for a walk or a commute
• Audio you have played stays on your phone, and reviews made offline sync when you reconnect

ANSWER OUT LOUD
• Hear the Arabic, then say what it means in English. The app grades your answer
• You answer out loud before the app shows the meaning
• Speech recognition runs on your device, after a 100 MB download
• Your voice is transcribed on your device and the audio is never uploaded. The text of your answer is saved

WORDS RETURN WHEN YOUR PREDICTED RECALL FALLS TO 90%
• FSRS scheduling adapts to how well you knew each word
• You choose 10, 20 or 30 new words a day
• Track your vocabulary, your streak and how long each word holds

Free. No ads. No subscription. An account is needed in the app.

Choose your Arabic face — Scheherazade, Amiri, Madina, Nastaliq or Noto — and read
in light, dark or sepia.
