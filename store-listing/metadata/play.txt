OpenArabic: Read the Classics

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80) ===
Read classical Arabic texts with English, native audio and spaced repetition.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000) ===
Read classical Arabic — even as a beginner.

OpenArabic gives you real works by real scholars — Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn Taymiyyah,
Ibn al-Jawzī, Imam Nawawī and more — with the English above each Arabic line. You
read the actual text, not a translation of it.

READ WITHOUT DECODING
• Every line of Arabic sits under its English, so you are never stuck on a sentence
• Real works on creed, fiqh, sīrah, ḥadīth and tafsīr — sorted by topic
• Graded by difficulty, from beginner-friendly to advanced
• Switch to Arabic-only when the help starts to feel slow
• The app keeps your place for next time

TAP ANY WORD YOU DON'T KNOW
• One tap gives you the meaning, a short grammar note and native audio
• See where the word comes from — its root, and the other words built on it
• Colour shows you the grammar: prefixes, suffixes, cases and particles
• Save it to practice without losing your place on the page

HEAR EVERY LINE READ ALOUD
• Queue any text and listen to it narrated, Arabic line then English
• Built for a walk or a commute
• Works offline once downloaded

ANSWER OUT LOUD
• Say the word instead of tapping a button — the app listens and grades you
• Harder to fool yourself, and you practise pronunciation at the same time
• Speech recognition runs on your device

WORDS RETURN BEFORE YOU FORGET THEM
• Saved words come back for review just before you would forget them
• FSRS scheduling adapts to how well you actually knew each word
• A few minutes a day is enough
• Track your vocabulary, your streak and how long each word holds

Free. No ads. No subscription.
