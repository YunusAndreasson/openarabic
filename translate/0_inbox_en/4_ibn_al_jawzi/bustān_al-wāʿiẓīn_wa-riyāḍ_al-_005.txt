It is narrated that Satan — may God's curse be upon him — said to Moses son of Amram, peace be upon our Prophet and upon him: "Never be alone with a woman who is not a close relative, for I will be the third among you. And never make a vow, lest I get the better of you. And when you intend to give charity, hasten to it — for if you do not, I will open before you seventy doors of poverty through which I will prevent you from giving."

It has been said regarding the words of God Almighty — *"Satan promises you poverty and commands you to indecency, while God promises you forgiveness from Him and bounty"* — that Satan draws you back to your own self and makes you forget your devotion to your Lord. It has also been said that he promises you poverty in seeking beyond what is sufficient: you may already have enough, yet you crave to accumulate more — and that craving is itself a poverty that never leaves. He turns you away from the richness of sufficiency toward the pursuit of excess, which is the ever-present poverty that leads its companion to severe and lasting torment. It has further been said that he promises you poverty in spending and giving for the sake of God Almighty — though such giving is true wealth, for God promises you forgiveness and bounty. Therefore, a servant should remember God's blessings upon him, His kindness toward him, and His generosity to him.

Know, O servants of God, that God Almighty has said in His clear revelation, upon the tongue of His Messenger Muhammad, peace be upon him: *"And whoever is guarded against the greed of his own soul — those are the truly successful."* Whoever is miserly and grasping is neither guarded nor successful.

Know that miserliness is a tree rooted in Hell, its branches hanging down over this world — it is the tree of Satan. Whoever clings to one of its branches, it drags him into the Fire. Likewise, generosity is a tree rooted in Paradise, its branches hanging down over this world. Whoever clings to one of its branches, it draws him into bliss. Generosity is among the qualities of the Noble King. Whoever embraces it has enraged the accursed Satan. The proof of this is that God Almighty never sent a prophet who was not generous, nor have you ever seen a righteous servant who was not generous. Generosity is the character of the prophets and the saints, and it is among the qualities of the Lord of all worlds. So practice it among yourselves, O community of believing men and women, O nation of Muhammad, the Seal of the Prophets.
