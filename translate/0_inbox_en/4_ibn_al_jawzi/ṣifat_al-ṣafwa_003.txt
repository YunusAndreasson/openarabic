When they stopped near Baḥīrā's hermitage — they had passed by him many times before without him ever speaking to them — that particular year they camped at a spot close to his cell where they had always made camp on their journeys. He prepared a meal for them and invited them all, something he had never done before. What moved him to do so was that, as they came into view, he noticed a cloud shading the Messenger of God ﷺ from among the entire caravan. When they settled beneath a tree, he watched the cloud settle over that same tree, its branches bending low over the Prophet ﷺ as he rested in its shade.

Seeing this, Baḥīrā came down from his cell and ordered the food to be brought out. He sent word to them: "People of Quraysh, I have prepared a meal for you, and I would like every one of you to attend — young and old, free and slave alike. Consider it a favor to me." One of the men said, "Something is going on with you, Baḥīrā. You have never done anything like this for us before. What has changed today?" He replied, "I simply wanted to honor you, and you deserve it."

They all gathered around him, but the Messenger of God ﷺ stayed behind with the baggage under the tree, being the youngest among them. When Baḥīrā looked over the group, he did not see the signs he knew and had read about in anyone present. He kept scanning the crowd but could not see the cloud over any of them — it remained hovering over the head of the Messenger of God ﷺ, who had stayed behind.

Baḥīrā said, "People of Quraysh, let no one be absent from my table." They told him, "No one has stayed behind except a boy — the youngest of us — left with the baggage." He said, "Call him to come eat. How wrong it would be for even one person to miss this, especially since I can see he is one of your own." The men replied, "By God, he is of the noblest lineage among us. He is the nephew of this man here" — meaning Abū Ṭālib — "and a descendant of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib." Al-Ḥārith ibn ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib said, "By God, it would be shameful of us to let a son of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib be left behind." He went to the boy, embraced him, and brought him to sit at the meal, the cloud still drifting above his head.

Baḥīrā watched him intently, studying features on his body that matched the description he possessed. When the meal was over, the monk approached him and said, "Boy, I ask you by al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā to answer what I ask you." The Messenger of God ﷺ replied, "Do not ask me by al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā — by God, there is nothing I despise more than those two." Baḥīrā said, "Then by God, tell me what I ask." He said, "Ask me whatever you wish."

The monk began questioning him about various matters, even about how he slept, and the Messenger of God ﷺ answered each question — every answer matching what Baḥīrā already knew. Then the monk looked between his eyes, and finally uncovered his back, where he saw the Seal of Prophethood between his shoulder blades, exactly as it was described in his texts.
