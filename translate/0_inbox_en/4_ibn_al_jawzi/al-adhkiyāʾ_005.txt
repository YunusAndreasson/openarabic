Ibn Abbas said: When Ishmael grew up, he married a woman from the tribe of Jurhum. Abraham came to visit but did not find Ishmael, so he asked his wife about him. She said, "He went out to seek provision for us." He then asked her about their living conditions, and she said, "We are in hardship and difficulty," complaining to him. He said, "When your husband returns, give him my greetings and tell him to change the threshold of his door." When Ishmael came back and she told him what had happened, he said, "That was my father, and he has instructed me to part ways with you. Go back to your family."

The author notes that this story also demonstrates Ishmael's perceptiveness.

Among the accounts related about Solomon, peace be upon him, is that he said: Two women went out, each with a child, and a wolf attacked and took one of the children. They began disputing over the remaining child and brought their case before David, peace be upon him, who ruled in favor of the older woman. As they passed by Solomon, peace be upon him, he asked what the matter was, and they told him the story. He said, "Bring me a knife — I will split the boy between you." The younger woman cried, "You would cut him in half?" He said, "Yes." She said, "No, don't! Let her have my share of him." Solomon said, "He is your son," and ruled in her favor.

Solomon, peace be upon him, once sent for one of the rebellious jinn to be brought before him. When the jinn arrived at Solomon's gate, he took a stick, measured it with his forearm, and threw it over the wall so that it landed before Solomon. Solomon asked, "What is this?" When he was told what the jinn had done, he said, "Do you know what he meant?" They said no. He said, "He is saying: Do whatever you wish, for in the end you will return to this much earth."

Abu Hurayrah said: While Solomon son of David, peace be upon him, was proceeding with his entourage, he passed by a woman calling out to her son, "Ya Ladin!" Solomon stopped and said, "The religion of God shall be made manifest." He sent for the woman and asked her about it. She explained that her husband had traveled and had a business partner. The partner claimed the husband had died and left instructions that if she bore a son, he should be named "Ya Ladin." Solomon sent for the partner, who confessed that he had killed the husband, and Solomon had him executed.

Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Qurazi said: A man came to the Prophet Solomon and said, "O Prophet of God, I have neighbors who steal my geese." Solomon called for a congregational prayer, then addressed the people in his sermon, saying, "One of you steals his neighbor's geese and then enters the mosque with feathers still on his head." A man instinctively wiped his head, and Solomon said, "Seize him — he is your culprit."

Among the accounts related about Jesus, peace be upon him, is that Iblis came to him and said, "Do you not claim that nothing befalls you except what God has decreed for you?" He said, "Indeed." Iblis said, "Then throw yourself off this mountain, for if safety has been decreed for you, you will be safe." Jesus replied, "O accursed one, God tests His servants, but it is not for the servant to test his Lord."
