God Almighty said: "When the earth is shaken with its earthquake." This is a Meccan surah, definitive in its promise and warning. God uses it to instill awe in His servants and to remind them of the earth's quaking and the coming of the Hour, so that they may cease the disobedience He forbade them from and comply with the obedience and faith He commanded of them.

He says: when the earth moves with all who are upon it, shaking from every direction, trembling from east to west, it will not cease until every mountain and structure on its surface is shattered. It will not settle until everything that emerged from it is swallowed back into its depths. Its quaking comes from the tremendous sound of Israfil, peace be upon him, and that will occur when the world's seasons, hours, months, moments, years, days, and all that is lawful and forbidden within it have come to an end.

That is when truth has been extinguished and falsehood has prevailed; when people have abandoned commanding good and forbidding evil; when they have committed sins and treated the forbidden as lawful; when injustice has spread among them; when jihad has been forsaken and corruption has surfaced; when usury has become rampant, sodomy and fornication have multiplied; when they have indulged in obscenities and wickedness and aided all of that with the drinking of wine; when people have commanded good but abandoned it themselves, and forbidden evil but committed it themselves; when they have despised the truth and followed their own desires; when the Quran has been recited but not acted upon; when hearts have blackened and indecencies and faults have multiplied; and when the corrupt have adorned themselves with sins and transgressions. When they have reached that state, the wrath of the Almighty, glorified and exalted, will intensify upon them. At that moment, God will say: "O Israfil, blow the blast of stupefaction." And Israfil will blow as the Almighty, majestic in His glory, has commanded him. The earth will then quake from its east to its west — and that is from a fury the Almighty unleashes upon the hypocrites and the wicked.

Israfil, peace be upon him, is a magnificent angel: one wing stretches to the east, another to the west; his feet extend beneath the lowest depths of the seventh earth by a distance of five hundred years; the seven heavens reach only to his knees; his neck is bent beneath the Throne, and the Throne rests upon his shoulders. He has extended his right leg and drawn back his left. The Preserved Tablet is between his eyes. He has placed the Trumpet to his lips, his gaze fixed toward the Throne, his ears attentive, waiting for the moment he is commanded to blow. The Trumpet is a horn of light. The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "The Trumpet is a horn of light, and by Him in whose hand is my soul, its greatest opening is as vast as the distance between heaven and earth." It is also reported that he said: "How can I be at ease when the bearer of the Trumpet has already placed it to his lips, bowed his forehead, fixed his gaze toward the Throne, and set his ears listening, waiting for the moment he is commanded to blow?"

When he blows it, all the inhabitants of the heavens and the earth will perish — except four angels, for they do not die until after all other creation has died: Gabriel, Michael, Israfil, and the Angel of Death. From the sheer force of Israfil's sound, the earth will convulse from east to west, and no structure will remain standing but that it collapses — except the mosques, for their foundations will endure and not be destroyed, on account of their honor before God Almighty, because He was worshiped and declared One within them, and His word was recited in them. That is the meaning of His saying, exalted be He: "Everything will perish except His Face." It has come in the exegesis that all things will perish except deeds done for the sake of God's Face, and the mosques will not perish because they were built solely for the sake of God Almighty.
