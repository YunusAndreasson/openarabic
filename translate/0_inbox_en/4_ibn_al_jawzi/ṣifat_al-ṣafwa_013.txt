Nafisa bint Munya said: Khadija bint Khuwaylid ibn Asad ibn al-Uzza ibn Qusayy was a woman of firm resolve, strong character, and noble standing. She was of the most central lineage among Quraysh and the wealthiest among them. Every man of her people was eager to marry her if he could. They had sought her hand and offered her great wealth, but she sent me secretly to Muhammad after he returned from Syria.

I said, "Muhammad, what keeps you from getting married?" He said, "I have nothing in my hands to marry with." I said, "What if that were taken care of, and you were invited to beauty, wealth, nobility, and a suitable match — would you not accept?" He said, "Who is she?" I said, "Khadija." He said, "How could that be arranged?" I said, "Leave that to me." He said, "Then I will."

So I went and told her, and she sent word to him: "Come at such and such an hour." She also sent for her uncle Amr ibn Asad to give her in marriage, and he came. The Messenger of God, peace be upon him, arrived accompanied by his uncles, and he married her. He was twenty-five years old, and Khadija was forty at the time.

Some scholars have mentioned that Abu Talib attended the marriage contract along with the clan of Mudar. Abu Talib said: "Praise be to God who made us of the descendants of Ibrahim, the seed of Isma'il, the roots of Ma'add, and the stock of Mudar. He made us the custodians of His House and the keepers of His sanctuary. He gave us a House that is a place of pilgrimage and a sanctuary of safety, and He made us rulers over the people."

"Now, this nephew of mine, Muhammad ibn Abdullah — no man is weighed against him without Muhammad outweighing him. If he is short on wealth, then wealth is a passing shadow and a fleeting thing. As for Muhammad, you already know his lineage. He has asked for the hand of Khadija bint Khuwaylid, and I have pledged her dowry, both immediate and deferred, from my own wealth. He is, by God, a man destined for great tidings and a momentous future."

And so the Messenger of God, peace be upon him, married her.
