It is mentioned that Abdullah ibn Sahl al-Tustari, may God have mercy on him, said: When Adam was expelled from Paradise — the abode of honor and safety — and brought down to the abode of humiliation, disgrace, trial, and tribulation, God Most High said: "O son of Adam, I settled you in My nearness, yet you disobeyed Me, obeyed Satan, and abandoned Me. By My might and majesty, I shall settle you in his nearness so that you may obey Me and disobey him, love Me and despise him. Then on the Day of Judgment, I shall say to you: obedience for obedience, and disobedience met with love — and then I shall admit you into Paradise."

It is reported in some traditions that when God Most High created Adam and his descendants, He placed four things in his heart: knowledge of God, intellect, faith, and certainty. Thus the heart became a treasury for these things. He then set four enemies upon the heart: Iblis, desire, the ego, and the world. Iblis guaranteed his companions that he would reach those treasures, as God said in His Book: *"I will come at them from before them and from behind them, and from their right and from their left."*

When the Lord, glorified and exalted, knew of the son of Adam's weakness and his inability to fend off these enemies, He taught him four of His names as a fortress against Iblis and his forces: O First, O Last, O Manifest, O Hidden. It is as though He said: "O son of Adam, I am the First — I will guard your knowledge of Me from what lies before you. I am the Last — I will guard your intellect from what lies behind you. I am the Manifest — I will guard your faith on your right. I am the Hidden — I will guard your certainty on your left."

Some of the wise were asked: "What is the wisdom in Iblis being given access to the son of Adam from four directions — from before him, from behind him, from his right, and from his left — but not being given access from above or below?" He answered: "Because those four directions are where human actions are subject to interference. But above is the place from which the Lord, glorified and exalted, looks upon the hearts of His believing servants, and below is the place where those who prostrate place their foreheads before the Lord of all worlds."

May God protect us and you from his temptation with a protection that admits us into His mercy, and may He accept our repentance and that of all sinners, for He is the Ever-Relenting, the Most Merciful. There is no power nor strength except through God, the Most High, the Most Great.
