Ataa ibn Yasar narrated that Moses, peace be upon him, said: "O Lord, who are Your people — those whom You shelter beneath Your Throne?" God said: "They are those whose hands are innocent and whose hearts are pure; those who love one another for the sake of My majesty; those who, when I am remembered, are remembered alongside Me, and when they are remembered, I am remembered with them; those who perform their ablutions thoroughly even when it is difficult; who return to My remembrance as eagles return to their nests; who are consumed with love for Me as a child is consumed with love for people; and who burn with rage when My sanctities are violated, as a leopard burns with rage when it is provoked."

Wahb ibn Munabbih narrated that when God sent Moses and his brother Aaron to Pharaoh, He said: "Do not be dazzled by his splendor or by what he has been given to enjoy, and do not cast your eyes longingly at any of it, for it is merely the bloom of this fleeting world, the adornment of the self-indulgent. Had I wished to dress you in such worldly finery that Pharaoh, upon seeing it, would realize his own power could never match what you were given, I would have done so. But I hold you above all that and keep it far from you — and this is what I do with all My friends. From the very beginning I have chosen what is best for them: I drive them away from the world's comforts and ease, just as a caring shepherd drives his flock from pastures of ruin. I steer them clear of its distractions and pleasures, just as a caring shepherd keeps his camels from disease-ridden resting grounds. This is not because they are lowly in My sight, but so that they may receive their full share of My honor — whole and undiminished, untouched by the world, uncorrupted by desire."

"Know that no adornment with which people may clothe themselves is more pleasing to Me than renunciation of this world. That is the adornment of the God-fearing. Upon them it is a garment by which they are recognized: tranquility and humility, their mark upon their faces from the trace of prostration. These are My true friends, truly and truly. So when you meet them, lower your wing to them, humble your heart and your tongue before them, and know this: whoever humiliates or frightens a friend of Mine has openly declared war against Me and challenged Me. He has offered himself up to Me and invited Me to take him — and I am the swiftest of all in coming to the aid of My friends. Does the one who wages war against Me think he can withstand Me? Does the one who opposes Me think he can overpower Me? Does the one who challenges Me think he can outrun Me or escape Me? How could he, when I am the one who avenges them in this world and the next, and I never entrust their defense to anyone but Myself?"
