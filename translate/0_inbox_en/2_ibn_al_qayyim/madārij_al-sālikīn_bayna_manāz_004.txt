There is yet another level of guidance — the final and highest of all levels — which is guidance on the Day of Resurrection to the path leading to Paradise. That is the Bridge that delivers one to it. Whoever was guided in this life to the straight path of God, the path upon which He sent His messengers and revealed His scriptures, will be guided there to the straight bridge that leads to His Paradise and the abode of His reward.

The firmness of a person's footing on that Bridge will correspond exactly to the firmness of their footing on the path God established for His servants in this life. And the speed of their crossing on that Bridge will match their pace on this path. Among them will be those who cross like lightning, those who cross in the blink of an eye, those who cross like the wind, those who cross as fast as a galloping mount, those who run, those who walk, and those who crawl. Some will be scratched but ultimately delivered, and others will be hurled into the Fire. So let every person gauge how they will fare on that Bridge by how they fare on this one — an exact correspondence, a fitting recompense: *"Are you repaid for anything but what you used to do?"*

Let them also consider the doubts and desires that hinder their progress on this straight path, for those are the very hooks lining both sides of that Bridge, snatching at them and holding them back from crossing. If such obstacles are many and powerful here, so too will they be there: *"And your Lord is not unjust to His servants."*

Thus, the prayer for guidance encompasses the attainment of every good and protection from every evil.
