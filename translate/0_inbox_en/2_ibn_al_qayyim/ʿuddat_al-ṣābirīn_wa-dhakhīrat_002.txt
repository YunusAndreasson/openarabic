To proceed: God, glorified be He, made patience a steed that never stumbles, a sword that never dulls, an army that is never defeated, and an impregnable fortress that can neither be demolished nor breached. Patience and victory are twin siblings — victory comes with patience, relief comes with hardship, and ease comes with difficulty. Patience aids its companion more than any number of men without weapons or armies, and its relation to triumph is like that of the head to the body.

The Faithful and Truthful One guaranteed those who practice patience, in the decisive verses of His Book, that He would reward them without measure. He informed them that He is with them through His guidance, His mighty support, and His clear victory, saying: *"And be patient, for God is with those who are patient."* Through this divine companionship, the patient ones attained the best of this world and the next, and through it they gained every blessing, inner and outer.

He, glorified be He, made leadership in religion contingent upon patience and certainty, saying — and by His words the rightly guided found their way: *"And We made from among them leaders who guide by Our command, for they were patient and had firm faith in Our signs."*

He declared that patience is best for those who practice it, affirming it with an oath: *"And if you are patient, it is better for those who are patient."*

He made known that with patience and mindfulness of God, the schemes of enemies cause no harm, even if those enemies hold power over you: *"And if you are patient and mindful of God, their scheming will not harm you in the least. God fully encompasses what they do."*

He told of His prophet Joseph the truthful — how his patience and mindfulness of God led him to a place of honor and authority: *"Whoever is mindful of God and is patient — God does not let the reward of the righteous go to waste."*

He made ultimate success dependent on patience and mindfulness of God, and the believers understood this well: *"O you who believe, be patient, outdo others in patience, stand firm, and be mindful of God, so that you may succeed."*

He spoke of His love for the patient, and in that lies the greatest encouragement for those who aspire: *"And God loves the patient."*

He gave the patient glad tidings of three things, each one greater than anything people of this world envy one another over: *"And give glad tidings to the patient — those who, when struck by misfortune, say: 'We belong to God, and to Him we return.' Upon them are blessings from their Lord, and mercy, and it is they who are rightly guided."*

He counseled His servants to seek help through patience and prayer against the trials of this world and of faith: *"Seek help through patience and prayer — it is indeed difficult, except for the humble."*

And He made entry into Paradise and salvation from the Fire something attained only by the patient: *"Today I have rewarded them for their patience — it is they who are the triumphant."*
