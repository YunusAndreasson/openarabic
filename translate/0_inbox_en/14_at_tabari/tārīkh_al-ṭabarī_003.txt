God said to the angels: "I am going to create a human being from clay. When I have shaped him and breathed into him of My spirit, fall down before him in prostration." So God created him with His own two hands, so that Iblis would have no grounds for arrogance toward him — so that when Iblis grew arrogant, God could say: "You are too proud for what I made with My own hands, while I Myself did not deem Myself above him!" He created him as a man, and he remained a body of clay for forty years, measured by the reckoning of Friday. The angels passed by him and were startled when they saw him. The most alarmed of them all was Iblis. He would walk by and strike the body, and it would ring out like pottery with a hollow clang. He said: "You were created for some purpose!" Then he entered through its mouth and came out from its back, and said to the angels: "Do not fear this one — your Lord is solid and eternal, but this thing is hollow. If I am given power over him, I will surely destroy him."

When the appointed time came that God willed to breathe the spirit into him, He said to the angels: "When I breathe into him of My spirit, prostrate before him." When He breathed the spirit into him and it entered his head, he sneezed. The angels said: "Say 'Praise be to God.'" So he said: "Praise be to God." And God said to him: "May your Lord have mercy on you."

When the spirit entered his eyes, he gazed upon the fruits of Paradise. When it entered his belly, he craved food. He leaped up before the spirit had even reached his legs, rushing eagerly toward the fruits of Paradise — and that is what is meant by the verse: "Man was created of haste."

Then the angels prostrated, all of them together — except Iblis. He refused to be among those who prostrated. He refused and was arrogant, and he was among the disbelievers. God said to him: "What prevented you from prostrating when I commanded you, to what I created with My own hands?" He said: "I am better than him. I would never prostrate to a mortal You created from clay." God said to him: "Then descend from here, for it is not fitting that you should be arrogant in it. Get out — you are among the debased." And debasement means humiliation.
