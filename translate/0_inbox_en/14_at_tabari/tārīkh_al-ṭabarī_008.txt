From Ibn Husayn — who was one of the Companions of the Messenger of God, peace be upon him — who said:

A group of people came to the Messenger of God, peace be upon him, and entered upon him. He began giving them glad tidings, but they kept saying, "Give us something," until this troubled the Messenger of God, peace be upon him. Then they left his presence, and another group came and entered upon him. They said, "We have come to greet the Messenger of God, peace be upon him, to gain understanding in the religion, and to ask him about the origin of all this." He said, "Then accept the glad tidings, since those who just left did not accept them." They said, "We accept." So the Messenger of God, peace be upon him, said: "God existed and there was nothing else besides Him. His Throne was upon the water, and He wrote everything in the Record before all things. Then He created the seven heavens." Then someone came to me and said, "Your she-camel has wandered off." So I went out, and the mirage was shimmering in the distance before her. How I wished I had just left her and stayed.

In another narration from Imran ibn Husayn, he said: The Messenger of God, peace be upon him, said, "Accept the glad tidings, O tribe of Tamim." They replied, "You have given us glad tidings, so give us something." He then said, "Accept the glad tidings, O people of Yemen." They replied, "We accept. Now tell us about this matter — how did it begin?" The Messenger of God, peace be upon him, said: "God, the Almighty, was upon the Throne. He existed before all things, and He wrote in the Tablet everything that would come to be." He said: Then someone came to me and said, "O Imran, your she-camel has slipped her tether." So I got up, and the mirage was shimmering between me and her, and I do not know what was said after that.
