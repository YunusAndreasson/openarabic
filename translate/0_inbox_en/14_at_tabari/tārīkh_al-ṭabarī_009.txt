The Jews came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: "O Muhammad, tell us — what did God create in these six days?" He replied: "He created the earth on Sunday and Monday, the mountains on Tuesday, and the cities, provisions, rivers, and all their flourishing and ruin on Wednesday. He created the heavens and the angels on Thursday, continuing until three hours remained of Friday. In the first of those three hours He created the lifespans, in the second He created calamity, and in the third He created Adam." They said: "You have spoken truly — if you finish." The Prophet ﷺ understood what they were getting at, and he grew angry. So God Most High revealed: *"And no weariness touched Us"* — *"So be patient with what they say."*

Abdullah ibn Salam said: "God began creation on Sunday. He created the earths on Sunday and Monday, provisions and the firm mountains on Tuesday and Wednesday, and the heavens on Thursday and Friday. He finished in the last hour of Friday, in which He created Adam in haste — and that is the very hour in which the Final Hour shall come."

Ibn Abbas said: "The first thing God Most High ever created was the Pen. He said to it: 'Write.' It said: 'What shall I write, O Lord?' He said: 'Write destiny.' So the Pen recorded all that would come to pass from that moment until the Day of Resurrection. Then He raised the vapour of the water and split the heavens from it. Then He created the great fish, and the earth was spread out upon its back. The fish stirred, and the earth shook, so it was anchored with the mountains — and indeed, the mountains take pride over the earth."
